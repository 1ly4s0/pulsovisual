How to make a video that reacts to music

  1. Open your track

    Pick audio from your device and hit play to see how the canvas reacts.

  2. Choose the look

    Start with Orbit, Club or Stardust. Adjust the glow and add a background image if you have cover art.

  3. Pick a size and save

    Use 16:9 for YouTube or 9:16 for Shorts, Reels and TikTok.

Frequently asked questions

Does the video include the track?

Yes. The video contains the track you opened and the animation from the visualizer.

Can I use a background image?

Yes. Pick a solid color or choose a background image from your device.

Is my file uploaded?

The file is processed in your browser. You don’t need an account.

How long does video export take?

It records while the clip plays, so export usually takes about as long as the clip. Choose MP4 with AAC audio or WebM.
